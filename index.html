i.  The House

A café built into a church.

Since the transformative refurbishment of the Narthex in the 1960s, All Saints' Church has been home to a warm welcoming café — a small, hospitable kitchen where the parish, the passing visitor and the office worker on a quick lunch break sit side by side.

The Bistro is helmed by a small, passionate team committed to serving you seven days a week. Inside the original stone walls there is cosy seating; out under the portico there are tables set in the open air, looking onto George Row.

Craig Ryan, proprietor

“An ideal ambience for unwinding, reconnecting with friends and family.”

What's on the counter changes with the day — but expect freshly baked scones for a proper cream tea, extra-large fluffy muffins still warm from the oven, jacket potatoes with assorted toppings, and rolls and sandwiches made to order.
